Define a Term for Two Audiences

Your Task

Part of the Homework portion of your course grade

Write a definition for two different audiences for one of the terms you’ve chosen for your User Document assignment.

You work as a Consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, a company that helps their clients improve their communication with customers, colleagues, and the public. See the full scenario for all the details on the writing situation.

Review Markel & Selber Ch 20 for help with this project.

Assignment Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Write a clear short definition.
  • Adjust the level of detail for different audiences.
  • Avoid unnecessary jargon.
  • Use examples to help readers understand unfamiliar terms.

Success Tips

  • Reuse part or all of your definition in your User Document assignment.
  • Use short sentences, chunked paragraphs, and other plain language techniques.
  • Follow the tips on Tengrrl’s Do’s & Don’ts for Workplace Writing page.
  • Check the number of words in your answer by going to the Tools menu and then choosing the Word Count command. Canvas will display a box with details on your answer.

No Use of AI

Do not use AI on this activity.

The goal is to be able to spot the differences yourself.

How You’ll Be Graded

Canvas will automatically give you credit for your submission.

I spot-check the work to ensure the grade is accurate.

If you shouldn’t have earned credit, I change the grade. This situation usually happens when answers are missing or incomplete. If time allows, I may ask you to try again.
