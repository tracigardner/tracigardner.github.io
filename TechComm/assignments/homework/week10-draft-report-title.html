Drafting Your Recommendation Report Title

Your Task

Part of the Homework portion of your course grade

Develop a clear working title for your Recommendation Report. Your title should identify the client document and help readers understand what to expect.

Use the five steps in the Build a Better Report Title infographic as you answer the survey questions.

Your title is a starting point. You can change it later as your research, findings, and recommendations take shape.

Success Tips

  • Keep the infographic open while you work.
  • Use the same client document you selected for your proposal.
  • Focus on clarity and specificity, not a perfect final title.

How You’ll Be Graded

Canvas will automatically give you credit for completing this activity. I spot-check responses and may adjust credit for missing or incomplete answers.
