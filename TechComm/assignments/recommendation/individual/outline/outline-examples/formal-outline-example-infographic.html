Follow the report's logic.

Methods explain how you evaluated the document; results present what you found; conclusions interpret the findings; recommendations propose action.

Use specific subpoints.

“Navigation Problems” is more useful than a vague heading such as “Other Issues.”

Don't force extra levels.

Add Level 4 only when a Level 3 point genuinely needs subdivisions.

Plan the whole report.

Your complete report will also have front and back matter. This example focuses on the body to demonstrate hierarchy.
