Recommendation Report Body Draft

Your Task

Part of the Homework portion of your course grade

Create and submit a PDF draft of the body of your Recommendation Report.

Your draft does not need to be polished or finished. The goal is to have as much as possible of the body completed before Thanksgiving break.

Your draft should include all five sections of the body (introduction, methods, results, conclusions, and recommendations). Some wording, research, annotations, visual details, and formatting can still need revision.

Your goal is progress, not perfection. A rough but complete draft earns full credit.

Assignment Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Develop a substantial draft of the body of a report.
  • Bring the major parts of the body together so you can evaluate how they work as a whole.
  • Make preliminary decisions about content, research, organization, visuals, and document design.
  • Identify areas that need revision before you submit the final report.

Success Tips

  • Get the body of your report onto the page. You still have time to revise and polish it.
  • Draft all the major sections. Do not spend so much time perfecting one section that the rest of the body remains unwritten.
  • Include your project visuals.
    • Ideally, add all three required visuals, even if you expect to revise them later.
    • If you don’t have a visual, include a placeholder image and a note that explains what you will add (for instance, I’ll add a bar chart of the readability statistics for different sections of hte client document).

No Use of AI

Do not use AI to draft or revise this checkpoint.

The goal is to bring your own ideas, research, and writing together into a substantial draft. You may use the optional AI feedback process later, as explained in the Recommendation Report assignment.

How You’ll Be Graded

This checkpoint is about progress, not evaluation. You’ll assess how much of the body of your report you have drafted, and your answer will determine your homework score automatically.

I will not grade the draft for quality or accuracy. I spot-check submissions to verify the self-assessment and adjust scores if needed.

Table 1. Grading Criteria
Points Your Draft
100 You submit a complete or nearly complete draft of the body of your report, including all five sections.
84 You submit a partial draft that clearly addresses the body of the your report assignment. You have written at least half of the body, but you are still missing substantial sections or required components.
74 You submit a partial draft that clearly addresses the body of the your report assignment. You have written less than half of the body.
0 You do not submit a draft.
