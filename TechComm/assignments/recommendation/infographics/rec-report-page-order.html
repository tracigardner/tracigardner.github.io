Before You Submit: Check the Assembly

  • All 12 required sections appear in the order shown; appendices are included only if needed.
  • The letter uses business letter format, and the title page displays the actual report title.
  • No pages or headings are labeled “Front Matter,” “Report Body,” “Back Matter,” “Letter of Transmittal,” or “Title Page.”
  • Front matter uses Roman numerals; the Introduction begins Arabic numbering at 1; numbering continues through the back matter.
  • Page breaks and table-of-contents entries match the finished document.
