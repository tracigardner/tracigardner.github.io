Recommendation Report:
Put the Pages in Order

A visual guide to section order, page breaks, and numbering

Build your report in the sequence below. The miniature pages show what belongs on the page; the labels and instructions beside them are not part of the report.

Use the actual report title and business letter format. Do not type “Letter of Transmittal” or “Title Page” as headings on those pages.
