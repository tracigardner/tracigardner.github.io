Why the Document Is Being Reviewed

New River Development Advisors prepared the study for Blue Ridge Residential Partners after a short period of research. It combines market assumptions, regulatory observations, infrastructure assumptions, a development budget, and a basic financial model. Blue Ridge Residential Partners’ acquisitions committee has complained that the draft is difficult to scan and that important assumptions, limitations, and unresolved questions are easy to overlook.

The review should consider opportunities to improve:

  • Document design and organization
  • Usability for busy decision-makers
  • Plain language and clarity
  • Accessibility

Your Role & Objective

As a consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, evaluate how effectively the study helps readers understand the proposal, distinguish evidence from assumptions, locate important information, recognize uncertainty, and make informed decisions about next steps.

Your proposal will explain why this document is an appropriate focus for your project, outline how you plan to investigate it, and request approval to proceed. Your later recommendation report will analyze the document in greater detail and recommend specific improvements supported by research and evidence.

Additional Context

This is an early-stage screening document. Some information has been checked; other points still require confirmation from surveyors, engineers, planners, utility staff, environmental consultants, lenders, brokers, and attorneys. A mistaken assumption about zoning, utilities, site conditions, lot yield, or cost could change the investment decision.

Evaluate the communication, not the proposed subdivision itself. Do not decide whether Cedar Ridge Estates should be built or redesign the development plan. Focus on changes that would make the study more useful to real decision-makers without simply making it longer.
