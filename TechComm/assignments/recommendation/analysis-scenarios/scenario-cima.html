Client Overview

Client Location:
Christiansburg, Virginia
Key Contact:
Jordan Lee, Volunteer Programs Coordinator

CIMA is a program of Christiansburg Institute, a community-based cultural heritage nonprofit. The museum preserves and interprets materials related to Christiansburg Industrial Institute and African American history in Southwest Virginia. Its audiences include former students and descendants, local residents, school groups, researchers, historians, genealogists, tourists, and other visitors.

Document Overview

Document:
CIMA Volunteer Guide to Inclusive Historical Communication
Purpose:
Community volunteers with varied backgrounds and little or no formal training in museum work, archival practice, or historical interpretation.
Primary Users:
Community volunteers with varied backgrounds and little or no formal museum, archival, or historical-interpretation training.
Use Context:
Volunteers may consult the guide while processing archival materials, drafting exhibit or digital content, selecting images, or responding to visitors.

Why the Document Is Being Reviewed

The guide was assembled from staff notes, earlier volunteer instructions, and contributions from several people. Staff believe the information is useful, but they are concerned that volunteers may have difficulty locating and applying guidance quickly during actual tasks.

CIMA wants the guide to function as a practical reference rather than a textbook. The review should consider opportunities to improve:

  • Document design and organization
  • Usability for volunteers completing real tasks
  • Plain language and clarity
  • Accessibility of the document itself

Your Role & Objective

As a consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, you will evaluate how effectively the guide supports volunteers who need clear, usable guidance while working with historical materials and public-facing communication.

Your proposal will explain why this document is an appropriate focus for your project, outline how you plan to investigate it, and request approval to proceed. Your later recommendation report will analyze the guide in greater detail and recommend specific improvements supported by research and evidence.

Additional Context

CIMA’s volunteers range from retirees and working adults to college students. Some have historical research experience; others are new to museums and archives. Staff review important work, but volunteers need enough guidance to make sound routine decisions without being overwhelmed by specialist terminology, lengthy policy explanations, or unnecessary detail.
