Choose one scenario for your Proposal and Recommendation Report project. You do not need to read every scenario in detail before deciding. Start by scanning Table 1, which gives a short description of each document and the disciplinary areas it may connect to. Open the detailed overview only for the scenarios that interest you most.

Choose based on the document or communication problem you find most interesting. You are not required to select a scenario related to your major, and you do not need prior expertise in the client’s field.
