Review a policy brief prepared for the Montgomery County Board of Supervisors and recommend ways to improve its design, clarity, usability, and accessibility before wider distribution.

Client Overview

Client:
Commonwealth Civic Strategies, LLC
Client Location:
Richmond, Virginia
Key Contact:
Elena Brooks, Senior Policy Consultant

Commonwealth Civic Strategies provides policy, planning, and public-sector advisory services to local governments in Virginia. The firm prepared a policy brief for Montgomery County officials as the county considers how future data center proposals should be evaluated through its existing planning and land-use processes.

Document Overview

Document:
Policy Options for Managing Data Center Development in Montgomery County, Virginia
Purpose:
Help county leaders compare approaches for reviewing future data center proposals and consider a consistent framework before a major application is submitted.
Primary Users:
Montgomery County Board of Supervisors members, county staff, and other officials involved in planning and land-use decisions.
Use Context:
The brief may be read electronically or in print before meetings, during policy discussions, and when staff begin drafting planning or zoning guidance.

Why the Document Is Being Reviewed

The brief was developed as county interest in data center policy increased. Its policy content is substantially complete, but Commonwealth Civic Strategies wants an outside review before the document is circulated more broadly to decision makers and staff. The firm is especially concerned with how quickly readers can locate, understand, and compare important information in a document covering technical, fiscal, environmental, and land-use issues.

The client wants to ensure that the document communicates effectively with its intended users and supports the organization’s goals. The review should consider opportunities to improve:

  • Document design and organization
  • Usability for the intended audience and context
  • Plain language and clarity
  • Accessibility

Your Role & Objective

As a consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, you will evaluate the client’s document and identify opportunities to make it more effective for its intended users.

Your proposal will explain why this document is an appropriate focus for your project, outline how you plan to investigate it, and request approval to proceed. Your later recommendation report will analyze the document in greater detail and recommend specific improvements supported by research and evidence.

Additional Context

The review is about how effectively the brief communicates, not whether Montgomery County should encourage or restrict data center development. The document addresses several issues that may matter to county officials, including fiscal effects, land-use compatibility, electricity and water demand, environmental impacts, transportation, public safety, and implementation. Recommendations should preserve the brief’s policy purpose while making the information easier for readers to navigate and use.
