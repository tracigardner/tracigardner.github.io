Appalachian Food & Farm Strategies, LLC (AFFS) is a small regional planning and economic development consulting firm. Its work connects agriculture, food systems, natural resources, rural entrepreneurship, and community development. AFFS prepared the plan for Thrive Food Access Network, an initiative of the Community Foundation of the New River Valley.

Document Overview

Document:
New River Valley Agribusiness & Local Food Development Plan
Purpose:
Provide a regional framework for future collaboration and investment in food processing, distribution, forest products, agritourism, institutional purchasing, and food access.
Primary Users:
Thrive leaders and regional partners, including producers, nonprofit leaders, local-government staff, economic-development professionals, funders, institutional buyers, and Extension personnel.
Use Context:
Readers may use the full plan for regional planning or consult individual sections when considering investments, partnerships, funding, or implementation decisions.

Why the Document Is Being Reviewed

AFFS considers the plan substantively useful, but the document may circulate beyond the people who helped develop it. Future readers may encounter it without a consultant available to explain its structure, recommendations, or assumptions. Before AFFS revises the plan or adapts its approach for future clients, the firm wants an outside review of how well the document works on its own.

The client wants to ensure that the document communicates effectively with its intended users and supports the organization’s goals. The review should consider opportunities to improve:

  • Document design and organization
  • Usability for the intended audience and context
  • Plain language and clarity
  • Accessibility

Your Role & Objective

As a consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, you will evaluate the plan and identify practical ways to make it more effective for the people expected to use it. Focus on communication rather than on whether the region should adopt a particular policy, investment, or economic-development strategy.

Your proposal will explain why this document is an appropriate focus for your project, outline how you plan to investigate it, and request approval to proceed. Your later recommendation report will analyze the document in greater detail and recommend specific improvements supported by research and evidence.

Additional Context

The plan covers Montgomery, Floyd, Giles, and Pulaski Counties and brings together several kinds of information, including regional needs, strategic initiatives, implementation steps, governance, budgets, measures, risks, and near-term actions.

AFFS can revise the writing, organization, page design, tables, graphics, navigation, and supporting information, but the review should not assume that the firm can conduct major new research or replace the underlying planning process.
