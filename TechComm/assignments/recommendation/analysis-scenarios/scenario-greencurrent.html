Client Request:
Evaluate GreenCurrent’s New Engineer Onboarding Guide and recommend revisions that make it easier for new engineers to find, understand, and use essential information during onboarding and everyday work.

Client Overview

Client:
GreenCurrent Civil & Environmental Engineering
Client Location:
Four offices in Virginia, North Carolina, and Maryland
Key Contact:
Sage Eagle, Director of Engineering Operations

GreenCurrent is a mid-sized civil and environmental engineering consulting firm with about 180 employees. The firm serves municipal governments, state agencies, utilities, developers, and conservation organizations on projects involving water resources, environmental conservation, and sustainable infrastructure.

Document Overview

Document:
New Engineer Onboarding Guide
Purpose:
Introduce new engineers to GreenCurrent’s values, professional expectations, and routine practices, while providing a reference they can consult after orientation.
Primary Users:
Newly hired engineers, especially recent graduates and engineers early in their careers.
Use Context:
During initial onboarding and later when employees need quick guidance about ethics, fieldwork, technical review, document control, regulatory compliance, sustainability, and related procedures.

Why the Document Is Being Reviewed

GreenCurrent is growing quickly and expects to hire about 30 employees in the next year, including many early-career engineers. Before the next group begins, the firm wants an outside review of how well the guide works as both an onboarding document and an ongoing reference.

The client wants to ensure that employees can locate and act on important information without unnecessary confusion, especially when the guide addresses technical procedures, safety, compliance, or fieldwork. The review should consider opportunities to improve:

  • Document design and organization
  • Usability for the intended audience and context
  • Plain language and clarity
  • Accessibility

Your Role & Objective

As a consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, you will evaluate the client’s document and identify opportunities to make it more effective for its intended users.

Your proposal will explain why this document is an appropriate focus for your project, outline how you plan to investigate it, and request approval to proceed. Your later recommendation report will analyze the document in greater detail and recommend specific improvements supported by research and evidence.

Additional Context

GreenCurrent’s work requires engineers to balance technical and regulatory requirements with budgets, schedules, public safety, environmental impacts, and community needs. New engineers may move between office work, field activities, data analysis, design, technical review, and communication with clients or regulators. The guide therefore needs to support both careful reading during onboarding and quick reference during later work.

GreenCurrent wants recommendations that improve the document without turning it into a comprehensive procedures manual; detailed project requirements and specialized procedures will remain in other company resources.
