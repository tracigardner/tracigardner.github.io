Draft Your User Document

Your Task

Part of the Homework portion of your course grade

Create and submit a PDF draft of your User Document: Learning Aid or Visual Glossary.

Your draft does not need to be polished or finished. The goal is to have enough of the document completed that you can spend the next week revising instead of starting.

Your goal is progress, not perfection. A rough but complete draft earns full credit.

Assignment Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Develop a substantial draft.
  • Bring the major parts of a document together so you can evaluate how they work as a whole.
  • Make preliminary decisions about content, organization, visuals, and document design.
  • Identify areas that need revision before you submit the final document.

Success Tips

  • Get the document onto the page. You still have time to revise and polish it.
  • Include the major content you expect to use, even if some wording still needs work or your draft is longer than one page.
  • Do not spend so much time perfecting one part that the rest of the document remains undrafted.
  • Use next week to revise content, design, plain language, accessibility, documentation, and correctness.

No Use of AI

Do not use AI on this activity.

The goal is to practice revising to improve your message. If AI revises for you, you aren’t practicing.

How You’ll Be Graded

You’ll assess how much of the User Document you have completed. Your answer will determine your homework score automatically.

Your score reflects how much you have drafted, not how polished the draft is. A complete draft can still include awkward wording, unfinished design decisions, temporary visuals, citation issues, or other features that you plan to revise.

Table 1. Grading Criteria
Points Your Draft
100 You submit a complete or nearly complete draft of the User Document assignment.
75 You submit a partial draft that addresses the User Document assignment.
0 You do not submit a draft.

I spot-check submissions to ensure the self-assessment reasonably matches the submitted draft. If it does not, I may adjust the score.
