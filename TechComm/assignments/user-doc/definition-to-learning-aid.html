From Definition to Learning Aid

A definition tells readers what something means. A learning aid helps them understand it, recognize it, and use it.

Step 1

Name It

Start with a clear definition.

Chunking

Breaking information into smaller, manageable units.

This tells readers what the term means.

Step 2

Explain It

Add enough context to make the idea understandable.

What is chunking?

Chunking divides dense information into smaller groups that are easier to scan, understand, and remember.

Now readers understand why the concept matters.

Step 3

Show It

Use a visual example so readers can recognize the concept.

Help Them Use It

Show readers when or how to apply the concept.

Build a Learning Aid, Not a Dictionary

A strong learning aid does more than define terms. It helps readers understand what a concept means, recognize it in practice, and apply it to their own work.

What is it?
Why does it matter?
What does it look like?
How can I use it?

Put It All Together

The four steps combine to create a learning aid that defines, explains, shows, and helps readers apply the concept.

Chunking: Make Information Easier to Use

Break dense information into smaller groups so readers can scan, understand, and act on it more easily.

What Is Chunking?

Chunking means breaking information into smaller, manageable units. It helps readers find information quickly and reduces the effort required to understand dense content.
