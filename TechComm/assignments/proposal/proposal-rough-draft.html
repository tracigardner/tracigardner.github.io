Draft Your Proposal
for Your Recommendation Report

Your Task

Part of the Homework portion of your course grade

Create and submit a PDF draft of your Proposal for Your Recommendation Report.

Your draft does not need to be polished or finished. The goal is to have enough of the proposal completed that you can spend the next week revising instead of starting.

Your draft should include the major parts of the proposal, including the proposal content, Gantt chart, additional visual, and annotated bibliography. Some wording, research, annotations, visual details, and formatting can still need revision.

Your goal is progress, not perfection. A rough but complete draft earns full credit.

Assignment Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Develop a substantial draft of your proposal.
  • Bring the major parts of the proposal together so you can evaluate how they work as a whole.
  • Make preliminary decisions about content, research, organization, visuals, document design, and the annotated bibliography.
  • Identify areas that need revision before you submit the final proposal.

Success Tips

  • Get the proposal onto the page. You still have time to revise and polish it.
  • Draft all the major sections. Do not spend so much time perfecting one section that the rest of the proposal remains unwritten.
  • Include your project visuals. Add a working Gantt chart and at least one additional relevant visual, even if you expect to revise them later.
  • Build the annotated bibliography now. Include your five sources and draft the citation, annotation, and two potential quotations for each source. You can refine the entries as you continue your research.
  • Use next week to revise. Focus on the proposal’s rationale, research plan, audience awareness, organization, design, plain language, visuals, source use, and correctness.

No Use of AI

Do not use AI to draft or revise this checkpoint.

The goal is to bring your own ideas, research, and writing together into a substantial draft. You may use the optional AI feedback process later, as explained in the Proposal assignment.

How You’ll Be Graded

This checkpoint is about progress, not evaluation. You’ll assess how much of the proposal you have drafted, and your answer will determine your homework score automatically. I will not grade the draft for quality or accuracy.

Table 1. Grading Criteria
Points Your Draft
100 You submit a complete or nearly complete draft of the Proposal assignment, including the major proposal sections, project visuals, and annotated bibliography.
75 You submit a partial draft that clearly addresses the Proposal assignment but is still missing substantial sections or required components.
0 You do not submit a draft.

I spot-check submissions to verify the self-assessment and adjust scores if needed.

Use the remaining week to revise your proposal. Final proposals submitted by the end of the grace period are eligible for revision as explained in the syllabus.
