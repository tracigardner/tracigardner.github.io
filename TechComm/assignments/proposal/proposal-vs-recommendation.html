Proposal vs. Recommendation Report

Same issue. Different stage. Different job.

Know Which Document You Are Writing

A proposal explains what you plan to investigate and why the investigation is worthwhile. A recommendation report presents what you learned and recommends specific actions based on evidence.

Proposal

A plan to investigate

Compare

Recommendation Report

Evidence-based findings and actions

Example Statement

“I will investigate whether the guide’s heading structure helps users locate procedures.”

Example

Example Statement

“Revise the guide’s headings so they describe the procedures beneath them.”

Purpose

Identify a problem or question and explain a plan for investigating it.

Purpose

Purpose

Present findings and recommend specific actions based on the evidence collected.

Evidence Available

Preliminary observations, credible outside sources, standards, guidelines, and a plan for gathering more evidence.

Evidence

Evidence Available

Findings from the completed investigation, such as document analysis, testing, comparisons, user feedback, and credible outside sources.

Common Verbs

investigate • examine • evaluate • assess • compare • research • analyze • test

Verbs

Common Verbs

recommend • revise • improve • add • remove • redesign • replace • reorganize

Level of Certainty

Tentative. You have enough evidence to justify an investigation, but you do not know the full answer yet.

Certainty

Level of Certainty

More definite. Your conclusions and recommendations are based on evidence you have gathered and analyzed.

What You Ask the Reader to Do

Approve the plan and authorize you to conduct the investigation.

Reader Action

What You Ask the Reader to Do

Consider, approve, prioritize, or implement the recommended changes.

The Key Difference: Where You Are in the Process

1 Observe a Possible Problem
2 Propose an Investigation
3 Gather & Analyze Evidence
4 Recommend Actions

Investigate First. Recommend After You Have Evidence.

In the proposal, make the case for what should be investigated and how you will investigate it. Save specific solutions for the recommendation report, when you have evidence to support them.
