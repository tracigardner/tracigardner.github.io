Overview

CAUTION: Do not work on this assignment until you have read the Informational Report assignment!

An annotated bibliography is not just a list of sources. It helps you decide which sources are credible, useful, and relevant before you begin writing the report. By summarizing each source, you also begin identifying the facts, examples, and expert perspectives that may support your report.

This preparation leads to stronger technical writing. A report is more compelling and convincing when its claims are supported by accurate evidence from reliable sources. The annotated bibliography helps you build that evidence early, organize your research, and avoid relying on weak or repetitive information.

This assignment asks you to begin gathering sources for your informational report, which will focus on one of the topics offered as options in the Informational Report assignment.

 Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Identify sources that may be useful for an informational report.
  • Practice summarizing sources clearly and accurately.
  • Gather information from different types of sources.
  • Prepare for a longer report by organizing your research early.
  • Distinguish between summary and personal opinion (objective vs. subjective, informational vs. argumentative)

 Types of Sources

Find five potential sources for your informational report. Your sources must include at least one print source, one online source, and one interview. The remaining two sources may be any appropriate source types.

Print Source

Examples can include a book, book chapter, printed report, textbook, or other published source available in print.

Online Source

Examples include a professional website, government website, organization website, online article, database source, or online report. Make sure that your source is reputable.

Interview

This should be an interview with someone who has knowledge of your topic. For a career pathway report, this might be someone working in the field. For an AI in the workplace report, this might be someone who uses, studies, manages, or makes decisions about AI in a workplace setting

 Source Content Requirements

Each of your sources must contain the following parts. Click on a tab to read about the requirement.

Part 1: Complete Citation

Set up your citation using the documentation style of your choice (MLA, APA, IEEE).

Here is an example of a citation set up in IEEE style:

“Understanding AI Literacy.” Stanford Teaching Commons, Stanford University, Available: https://teachingcommons.stanford.edu/teaching-guides/artificial-intelligence-teaching-guide/understanding-ai-literacy

Do not indent the citation. Start it at the left margin.

Part 2: Annotation

Summarize the source in clear, plain language. Explain:

  • What the source is about
  • What main information it provides
  • What specific facts, examples, topics, or ideas it discusses
  • How it connects to your informational report

Your summary must be objective. Do not include opinion. Avoid phrases like 

  • "In my opinion"
  • "I believe"
  • "It seems that"

Here is a sample annotation of the article shown in the Part 1 tab:

This module uses a specialized framework to help teachers develop AI literacy skills that can be shared with their students. This literacy framework focuses on four areas: functional, ethical, rhetorical, and pedagogical. Each area is defined, with examples, and resources are provided for each. The module also uses what it calls a “human-centered approach” to teaching AI literacy, which places humans in control of interactions. The site also provides teachers with practice exercises that consist of scenarios to which they can respond.

Notice that you do not indent your annotation.

Part 3: Two Potential Quotations

Look for two interesting or thought-provoking quotations from each source that you could potentially use in your report.

Double space after the annotation and bullet list two quotations, like this:

  • “In centering human agency, we recognize individual and collective responsibility and accountability concerning the appropriate use of AI technology, which includes mitigating negative impacts and assuring equitable benefits for all.”
  • “A supportive learning environment can help students develop habits that leverage metacognitive reflection, self-examining what they have learned and how they have learned it to identify when they access their zone and learn.”

 Assignment Requirements

  • Genre: Your bibliography should take the form of a memo, addressed from you to me. In this case, you need only a short introduction, something like Below you will find the five sources I am considering for my informational report.
  • Length: 2-4 pages of text
  • Format: Genre format requirements; 1" margins all around; page numbering (your choice of placement)
  • Design: Headings (if needed); single-spacing within paragraphs; double-spacing between paragraphs and around headings
  • Writing Style: plain language techniques (e.g., short sentences, chunked paragraphs)
  • Submission: Submit as a PDF to this page
