Correspondence & Informational Report

Your Task

Worth 15% of your course grade

Write a memo to a client that provides a brief, objective informational report on TWO similar tools they have asked about. Present neutral facts without advocating for any specific option or choice.

You work as a Consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, a company that helps their clients improve their communication with customers, colleagues, and the public. See the full scenario for all the details on the writing situation.

Review Markel & Selber Ch 14 and 17 for help with this project. Check your work with the Correspondence & Informational Report Self-Check as you work.

Assignment Learning Objectives

After working on this assignment, you should be able to:

  • Write a short informational report for a professional audience.
  • Explain an issue or trend in plain language.
  • Gather information from print sources, online sources, and interviews.
  • Use headings and document design to organize information clearly.
  • Summarize research without turning the report into an argument.
  • Format a short report as a professional memo.
  • Practice appropriate professional correspondence etiquette, tone, and formatting to build credibility with your reader.

Your Audience

Write your message for Luci Lee, Director of Research and Development at your client, W. A. Caldwell & Associates. Lee and her team will use your report to decide what tools will work best for their organization.

Assignment Requirements

  • Genre: Workplace-ready memo. See the details and examples in Chapter 14 of Markel & Selber (pp. 386–388).
  • Length: 1–2 pages of text. Longer documents okay.
  • Layout: 1" margins all around. Number pages 2 to the end. No page number on the first page.
  • Document Design:
    • Single-space within paragraphs.
    • Skip one blank line between paragraphs and around headings (double space).
    • Do not indent paragraphs.
    • Chunk paragraphs.
    • Use hierarchical headings to structure the body.
  • Plain Language: Use short sentences and other plain language techniques.
  • Submission: Submit as a PDF to this assignment page.

Success Tips

  • Follow the examples in the textbook for correct memo format.
  • Consult the full scenario for background and additional details on the task.
  • Don’t be a cheerleader! Use a clear and objective tone.
  • Use the hierarchical headings to organize your memo. Add no headings to the introduction or closing paragraphs.
  • Use all the tips on Tengrrl’s Do’s & Don’ts for Workplace Writing page.
