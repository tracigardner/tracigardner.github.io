Background: W. A. Caldwell & Associates is a regional consulting firm that manages major municipal, state, and federal contracts. Because their deliverables rely on public funding and governmental partnerships, all digital products and services must strictly comply with state and federal accessibility standards, including Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act and WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines.

The Challenge

A recent internal communications audit revealed significant accessibility gaps across Caldwell & Associates' digital documents. Staff members currently rely on ad-hoc formatting for public reports, policy briefs, and client deliverables, resulting in recurring errors in:

  • Document headings and logical reading order
  • Screen-reader compatibility and structural tags
  • Color contrast and visual hierarchy
  • Alternative text (alt text) for images, charts, and figures

To resolve these issues, Caldwell & Associates plans to standardize its document workflow and implement a mandatory accessibility training program for all employees.

Your Role & Objective

As a Communications Consultant at Maroon + Orange Digital, you have been brought in to help Caldwell & Associates select the right tools and establish a sustainable accessibility workflow.

Luci Lee, Director of R&D, has compiled a candidate list of document testing tools and software programs. She needs your team to evaluate these tools and deliver a detailed, comparative report. Her leadership team will use your report to decide which tools to procure and what training program to implement.

Key Evaluation Criteria

When preparing your evaluation, ensure your analysis addresses the following priorities:

  • Compliance Verification: How effectively each tool identifies violations against Section 508 and WCAG standards.
  • Usability & Adoption: How easily non-technical staff can integrate the tool into their daily drafting process.
  • Software Integration: Compatibility with common office software (e.g., word processors, PDF editors, web browsers).
  • Actionable Feedback: Quality of error reporting and guidance provided to help users fix identified issues.
