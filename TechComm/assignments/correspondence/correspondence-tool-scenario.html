Overall Project Goals

Maroon + Orange Digital (M+OD) recently conducted a communications audit of Caldwell & Associates (C&A) that revealed significant accessibility gaps across C&A’s digital documents. The company’s staff members currently rely on ad-hoc formatting for public reports, policy briefs, and client deliverables, resulting in recurring errors in:

  • Document headings and logical reading order
  • Screen-reader compatibility and structural tags
  • Color contrast and visual hierarchy
  • Alternative text (alt text) for images, charts, and figures

To resolve these issues, we’re working with C&A to standardize its document workflow and implement a mandatory accessibility training program for all employees.
