Fit It on One Page

Plan before you shrink. If your learning aid does not fit on one page, fix the content and structure before making everything smaller.

Step 1

Too Much

A crowded page usually means the document is trying to do too much.

The problem is not the page size. The page is trying to do too much.
Step 2

Prioritize

Decide which information actually earns space on the page.

Must Have

  • Clear purpose
  • Core concepts
  • Useful explanation
  • Meaningful visual
  • Help applying the ideas

Helpful

  • Extra example
  • Additional detail
  • Background information

Cut or Combine

  • Repeated explanations
  • Long introductions
  • Extra concepts
  • Decorative content

When Your Page Is Too Full

Try these strategies in order before you start shrinking.

1
Cut repetition and unnecessary background
2
Shorten long explanations
3
Combine related information
4
Show information with a useful visual or list
5
Narrow the number of concepts

Shrinking Comes Last

Don’t solve a content problem by creating a readability problem. Keep the required font, margins, spacing, and other specifications intact.

Don’t make everything smaller. Make the content smarter.
