Your Project Feedback Process

Major projects follow the same feedback process for everyone: prepare, submit, revise if needed, and resubmit.

1
Before You Submit

Prepare & Check

Use the assignment, rubric, examples, self-checks, and other course resources to evaluate your work.

Need help? Ask a specific question about a requirement, decision, or approach.
2
First Assessment

Submit Your Project

Submit your complete project by the end of the Grace Period. I assess it using the assignment requirements and rubric.

Earn 90 or higher?
You’ve met the standard. You’re finished.
3
If You Earn Below 90

Revise

Use the feedback from your first assessment to strengthen the project before the Revision Deadline.

Focus your revision on the areas identified in the rubric and feedback.
4
Revision Submission

Resubmit

Submit your revised project for another review. You can continue revising until you earn 90 or the Revision Deadline arrives.

Revision grades are capped at 90.

Specific Questions Are Always Welcome

I’m happy to help while you work. Ask whether you understand a requirement correctly, whether an approach fits the assignment, or whether a particular choice makes sense for your audience.

Why I Don’t Pre-Grade Complete Drafts

Reviewing a complete draft before submission would add another round of assessment. Because I cannot provide that extra assessment equally to everyone, complete-project assessment begins after submission.

Help before submission. Assessment after submission.
