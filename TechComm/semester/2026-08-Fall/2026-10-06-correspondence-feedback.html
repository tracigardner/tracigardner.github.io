I have added feedback on all of the Correspondence & Informational Reports. In this post, I’ll share details on your grades and how to use the feedback to improve your writing in the course.

Feedback on Your Correspondence & Informational Report

Find comments on your Correspondence & Informational Reports in three possible places:

  • Annotations on your submission. Look for comments on or to the left of your document.

  • Comments in the rubric. Read the description listed in the rubric as well as any custom comments added for specific characteristics.

  • Summary comments that show when you look at the assignment.

Revision for the Correspondence & Informational Report

If you earned an 89 or less on your Correspondence & Informational Report, you can revise your project and resubmit your work to this assignment. The grade for your revision will replace the current grade, but it is capped at 90. If you decide not to revise, your grade will stand as is.

Correspondence & Informational Report Revision Dates

  • The deadline for revision of your Correspondence & Informational Report is Tuesday, October 13 at 11:59 PM.

  • The grace period for Correspondence & Informational Report revisions is Friday, October 16 at 11:59 PM.

How to Resubmit

If you eligible to submit a revision, go to the Correspondence & Informational Report, and upload your new attempt.
