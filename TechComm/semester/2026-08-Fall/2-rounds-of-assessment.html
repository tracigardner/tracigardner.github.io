Your Project Feedback Process

Major projects follow the same process for everyone: prepare, submit, revise if needed, and resubmit.

Stage 1

Before You Submit: Build, Check, and Ask

Start by using the assignment, rubric, and examples to evaluate your work. Then use the feedback options below if you need more help.

Feedback Before You Submit

Visit the Writing Center

Get feedback at any stage, from ideas and outlines to a complete draft. Make an appointment through WCOnline.

Complete the Self-Check

Compare your draft to the project requirements and identify areas to revise.

Try an AI Draft Review

Use the Self-Check prompt to have a chatbot compare your draft with the assignment expectations.

Search HokieBot’s Notebook

Ask questions about the assignment and its requirements in the course’s Gemini Notebook.

Ask Traci

Ask me specific questions about the assignment or choices you’re making. I’ll read your project after you submit it to ensure everyone has the fair access to feedback.

Stage 2

First Assessment: Submit Your Project

Submit your complete project by the end of the Grace Period. I assess it using the assignment requirements and rubric.

Submit Turn in the complete project.
Assessment I review the project using the rubric.
Feedback You receive a grade and comments.

Check Your Feedback and Decide What to Do Next

Earn 90 or Higher You’ve met the standard. You’re finished.
Earn Below 90 Move to the revision stage.
Stage 3

Revision: Use the Feedback

If your project earns below 90, use the first assessment to identify what needs work and strengthen the project.

Review Read the rubric and feedback carefully.
Focus Identify the areas that need improvement.
Revise Make meaningful changes to the project.
Stage 4

Revision Submission: Resubmit

Submit the revised project for another review before the Revision Deadline.

Resubmit Turn in the improved version.
Continue if Needed You may continue revising until you earn 90 or the Revision Deadline arrives.
