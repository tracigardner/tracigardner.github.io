Two Built-In Stages of Instructor Feedback

1
First Stage

Submit for Assessment

Submit your complete project by the end of the Grace Period. I assess it using the assignment requirements and rubric.

Your complete project receives an instructor review.
You receive a grade and feedback explaining how the project meets the expectations.
Earn 90 or Higher You’ve met the standard. You’re finished.
Earn Below 90 Use your feedback and move into the revision period.
Revise
if eligible
2
Revision Stage

Improve & Resubmit

If your project earns below 90, use the feedback from the first assessment to strengthen your work before the Revision Deadline.

Review the rubric comments and identify what needs work.
Make meaningful revisions rather than simply correcting isolated errors.
Resubmit during the revision period for another review.

The Revision Period Gives You Room to Improve

Eligible projects can be revised until you earn 90 or the Revision Deadline arrives, whichever comes first.

Review Feedback See what is working and what needs attention.
Revise Strengthen the content, writing, design, or other criteria.
Resubmit Turn in the improved version before the deadline.

Why Complete Drafts Aren’t Pre-Graded

A complete draft review before submission would add another round of assessment. Some students could finish early enough to request that extra review while others could not. Because I cannot provide that additional assessment equally to everyone, complete-project assessment begins after submission.

Specific Questions Are Always Welcome

Not pre-grading does not mean you cannot ask for help. Ask about a specific requirement, decision, or approach while you work.

“Am I interpreting this requirement correctly?”
“Does this approach fit the assignment?”
“Would this type of visual make sense for my audience?”

Help Before Submission. Assessment After Submission.

Use the course resources and ask specific questions while you work. Then use instructor feedback and the revision process to improve your project when needed.
