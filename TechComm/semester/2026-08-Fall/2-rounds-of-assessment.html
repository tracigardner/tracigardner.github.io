Why Complete Drafts Aren’t Pre-Graded

Reviewing a complete draft before submission would add another round of assessment. Because I cannot provide that extra assessment equally to everyone, complete-project assessment begins after submission.

Stage 2

First Assessment: Submit Your Project

Submit your complete project by the end of the Grace Period. I assess it using the assignment requirements and rubric.

Submit Turn in the complete project.
Assessment I review the project using the rubric.
Feedback You receive a grade and comments.
Earn 90 or Higher You’ve met the standard. You’re finished.
Earn Below 90 Move to the revision stage.
Stage 3

Revision: Use the Feedback

If your project earns below 90, use the first assessment to identify what needs work and strengthen the project.

Review Read the rubric and feedback carefully.
Focus Identify the areas that need improvement.
Revise Make meaningful changes to the project.
Stage 4

Revision Submission: Resubmit

Submit the revised project for another review before the Revision Deadline.

Resubmit Turn in the improved version.
Continue if Needed You may continue revising until you earn 90 or the Revision Deadline arrives.
