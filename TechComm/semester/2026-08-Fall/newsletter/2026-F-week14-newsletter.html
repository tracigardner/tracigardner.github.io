Can Your Report Pass the Skim Test?

Your Recommendation Report may be 15 pages long, but a busy client might spend only a minute deciding where to focus. Will your report help them find what matters?

Before submitting, try this quick usability test: Skim your report without reading the paragraphs. Look only at headings, visuals, captions, and highlighted information.

1. Scan the Headings

Can you identify the major problems and recommendations from the headings alone? Or are they too general to be useful?

2. Inspect the Visuals

Do screenshots, tables, and figures make the evidence easier to understand? Do their captions explain what readers should notice?

3. Find the Takeaway

Can a reader quickly locate your most important recommendation and understand what action the client should take?

If you struggle to find information in your own report, imagine how difficult it might be for someone seeing the document for the first time. Use what you discover to make a few targeted improvements before submitting.

Remember: Your report should model the same reader-centered communication practices you recommend to your client.
